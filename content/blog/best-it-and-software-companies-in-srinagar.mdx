## 5 Critical Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Software Company in Srinagar

Before signing a contract or paying an advance to any IT agency in Srinagar, ask prospective vendors these five critical questions:

### 1. "What exact technology stack will you use to build our platform?"
If an agency answers with "WordPress," "Joomla," or unversioned PHP scripts, exercise extreme caution. These legacy tools suffer from sluggish load times (>3.5 seconds on mobile), frequent security plugin vulnerabilities, and severe limitations when customizing complex business workflows.

A forward-thinking engineering firm should be building with:
- **Frontend:** Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, TailwindCSS.
- **Backend:** Node.js, Python (FastAPI/Django), or Go for lightweight, high-concurrency microservices.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL or MongoDB with Redis caching layers.
- **Containerization:** Docker containers deployed on dedicated cloud infrastructure (AWS, Google Cloud, DigitalOcean).

### 2. "Will this software continue working if the internet goes down?"
In Kashmir, offline resilience is not a luxury; it is a fundamental operational requirement. Ask the engineering team if they utilize Progressive Web App (PWA) service workers, local IndexedDB caching, or client-side SQLite databases. If a system cannot process invoices, scan barcodes, and record payments offline, it will fail during critical operational windows.

### 3. "Do we retain 100% ownership of our source code and database?"
Beware of agencies that retain your code on their proprietary servers, effectively holding your business hostage to perpetual monthly licensing fees. An ethical, enterprise-grade engineering partner provides:
- Complete transfer of your GitHub/GitLab source code repository upon project completion.
- Full administrative access to your private database and cloud hosting accounts.
- Zero per-user monthly subscription fees.

### 4. "Do you have local engineers who can visit our office in person?"
Distant remote agencies based in Delhi, Chandigarh, or Bengaluru often promise the world during sales calls, only to disappear into endless email ticket queues when critical bugs arise. Having an engineering team with active operations in Srinagar means:
- Senior architects can visit your office, warehouse, or retail floor in person.
- Your non-technical staff receive hands-on training on their actual workstations.
- Urgent operational issues can be addressed with same-day face-to-face accountability.

### 5. "What performance guarantees and Core Web Vitals scores do you commit to?"
Over 80% of digital traffic in Srinagar originates on smartphones. Google strictly ranks web platforms based on **Core Web Vitals**: Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) must be under 2.5 seconds, and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) must be under 0.1. A top-tier firm should guarantee sub-500ms server response times and 90+ Google Lighthouse scores.
