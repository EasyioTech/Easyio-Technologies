## 5 Critical Factors to Review Before Hiring an IT Firm in Srinagar

Before signing a contract or transferring an advance deposit to any software company in Kashmir, demand full clarity on these five operational factors:

### 1. Unconditional Source Code & IP Ownership
Ensure your agreement explicitly guarantees that your company owns **100% of the source code, database architecture, and intellectual property**. Some agencies retain code ownership and license the software back to you, leaving you stranded if you ever choose to change vendors. With Easyio Technologies, all intellectual property is transferred directly to your organization upon project completion.

### 2. Verified Page Load Speeds on Mobile 4G/5G Networks
More than 70% of web traffic in Jammu & Kashmir is accessed on smartphones. Request live URLs of projects previously completed by the agency and run them through Google PageSpeed Insights. If an agency's demo sites score below 80 or take longer than 3 seconds to load on mobile connections, your customers will abandon your site before it finishes rendering.

### 3. Graceful Offline Handling for Local Network Realities
Ask prospective developers: *"What happens to our billing counter or dispatch manifest when the broadband connection goes down?"* If their answer is that the system simply requires an active connection, they do not understand the operating environment of Kashmir. Insist on offline caching, local data queuing, and automated bi-directional synchronization.

### 4. Direct Communication with Engineers, Not Sales Middlemen
One of the most common complaints among business owners in Srinagar is dealing with non-technical account managers who overpromise features and fail to communicate requirements to their offshore developers. Work with teams where you have direct access to lead software architects who understand both your business model and the underlying code.

### 5. Clear Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and Emergency Response Times
Software requires continuous operational maintenance, security patches, and periodic feature enhancements. Verify that your technology partner provides written SLAs outlining specific response times (e.g., critical bugs resolved within 4 hours; general requests within 24 hours).
