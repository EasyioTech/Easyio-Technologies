## 4 Critical Factors to Consider Before Hiring an IT Firm in Srinagar

Before signing a contract with any software development company in Kashmir, demand clarity on these four elements:

1. **Who Owns the Source Code?**
   Ensure your contract explicitly states that your business owns 100% of the repository, database schema, and intellectual property without licensing fees if you ever decide to switch providers.
2. **Page Speed & Mobile Responsiveness:**
   Over 70% of web traffic in Kashmir originates from mobile devices. If an agency's demo site takes longer than 3 seconds to load on a 4G connection, your customers will bounce before seeing your products.
3. **Offline Resilience & Data Backups:**
   Ask your technology partner how their application behaves when internet connection drops. Professional platforms incorporate service workers, local SQLite/IndexedDB caching, and automatic synchronization.
4. **Post-Launch SLA (Service Level Agreement):**
   Clear emergency response times and bug-fix commitments are essential for operational continuity.
