### 1. Controlled Atmosphere (CA) Cold Storages & Apple Mandis
The Lassipora Industrial Growth Centre in Pulwama and the fruit mandis of Shopian, Kulgam, and Anantnag form the backbone of Kashmir's cold-chain infrastructure. Managing thousands of metric tons of apples across multiple refrigerated chambers requires specialized logistics software:
- **Chamber Telemetry & Pallet Mapping:** 2D visual floor mapping of crates, tracking harvest dates, chamber oxygen/CO2 concentrations, and rental billing slabs.
- **Arath Commission Accounting:** Automated management of grower advances (*biyana*), commission percentages, labor loading fees (*palledari*), and transit manifests for trucks traveling along the Srinagar-Jammu National Highway (NH44).
- **Grower Transparency:** Automated WhatsApp receipts sent to growers upon fruit deposition and grading, establishing trust and eliminating manual ledger disputes.

### 2. High-Density Wholesale & Retail Distribution
Along KP Road, Reshi Bazar, and the Mehendi Kadal commercial strips in Anantnag, distributors process hundreds of retail transactions daily. 

Key technical requirements include:
- **Offline-First POS Operations:** Periodic network fluctuations in high-altitude zones must never halt checkout counters. Easyio’s software operates locally in offline mode, automatically synchronizing data with cloud databases the moment connection resumes.
- **Batch & Expiry Tracking:** Critical for pharmaceutical distributors and food wholesalers who need automated alerts before stock expires, preventing inventory write-offs.
- **Multi-Depot Stock Synchronization:** Real-time visibility between central warehouses in Lassipora and retail outlets in Anantnag town.

### 3. Healthcare & Diagnostic Clinics
KP Road in Anantnag hosts South Kashmir's largest cluster of private healthcare clinics, nursing homes, and diagnostic laboratories. Modern healthcare administration demands Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) compliant electronic medical records, automated WhatsApp diagnostic test delivery, and frictionless doctor appointment queues.
